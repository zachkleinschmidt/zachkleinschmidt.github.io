Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and protect information when you use our Discord bot/application (the “Service”).

1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following information:

  • Discord user ID, username, and discriminator
  • Server (guild) ID and server information the bot is in
  • Channel IDs and message content only when required for the bot’s functionality
  • Interaction data (slash commands, buttons, etc.)
  • Basic usage analytics (optional)

We do not collect passwords, payment information, or sensitive personal data beyond what Discord provides.

2. How We Use Information

Information is used solely to:

  • Provide and operate the bot’s features
  • Respond to commands and interactions
  • Improve the Service
  • Comply with legal obligations

3. Data Storage & Retention

Data is stored only as long as necessary to provide the Service. Message content is not stored permanently unless required for a specific feature. We do not sell or share your data with third parties.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information. Data may be shared only if required by law or to protect the rights and safety of users.

5. Your Rights

You may request deletion of data associated with your Discord account by contacting us. You can also remove the bot from your server at any time.

6. Contact

For privacy questions or data requests, contact: godrecallinquiries@gmail.com

This policy applies to our Discord application only.
